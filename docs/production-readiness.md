## Critical launch blockers and next actions

1. Approve and provision a production-like HTTPS staging host, scoped SendGrid account/key, signed public webhook and authenticated sending domain. Verify the actual external responses; local fakes cannot satisfy this.
2. Complete multi-worker failure/restart/load exercises, operational alerting, encrypted backups and a recorded restore drill. Review target-host security/log redaction and independent high-risk findings.
3. Verify the controlled single-recipient simulation/sandbox test workflow, actual Outlook/Gmail output and client usability acceptance.
4. Client must approve contact source/lawful basis, privacy notices, tracking and retention policy. Leave tracking off until then.
5. Authorise the small pilot in controlled-pilot.md, record real evidence for all 18.9 rows, review results and obtain separate approval before wider sending.
